Record your judgment

Opening this packet as a file lets you download a review. Open it through the local review server to save directly to the trial.

Enter your name or a stable reviewer alias, such as “Albert” or “Reviewer A”. Use the same identifier across trials. This identifies the person judging the evidence.

Success: the recorded evidence shows that the agent satisfied the full task. For search, it correctly identifies the requested object; for sequential tasks, all stages are satisfied in order. Failure: sufficient evidence shows that the full task was not satisfied by the end of the trial. Unknown: the recording or task criterion is insufficient to decide. Proximity or geometric visibility alone does not prove success. For exploration, apply the agreed exploration criterion; if none exists, choose Unknown.

Cite the exact iteration/image IDs or simulation timestamps, describe what they show, and explain why they support your result. For sequential tasks, cite each stage and its order. For Unknown, describe what is missing. Example: “Iteration 000004/image_000.jpg shows the requested chair; the applied answer identifies it and finishes the task. The earlier images do not show it.” Cite applied answers; a discarded prefetched result alone is insufficient.

Correct: the agent declared the task complete and the evidence supports that declaration. Incorrect: it declared completion but the evidence contradicts it (false completion). Not assessed: no final declaration exists, or its correctness cannot be judged. A timeout is not a completion declaration. This is separate from whether the task was satisfied.

Leave unchecked for a first review. Check only when you intentionally revise a saved judgment. Saving without this checkbox protects an existing review.
